FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How do I get a pilot code?

RostiQ is in its pilot phase, so new teams start with a free pilot code. Email levi@caersultancy.com with your team's name and sport, and you'll usually get your code the same day.

Is RostiQ really free?

Yes. During the pilot everything is free, no credit card. If paid extras for clubs come later, you'll hear well in advance.

Do my players have to install an app?

No. They open the link you share in the group and sign in once with Google or a link by email. Whoever wants can put RostiQ on their home screen.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in every modern browser: Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge. On phone, tablet and computer.

Who sees our data?

Only the members of your team. The data is stored in the EU (Belgium), we show no ads and sell nothing on. Your mobile number isn't shown anywhere on the site. More in our privacy statement.

What if someone doesn't use WhatsApp?

No problem: RostiQ works entirely through the link. WhatsApp is just handy for sharing messages.

Can RostiQ use our club colours?

Yes. As an admin you choose your own logo, colours and font. Your players see your house style right away.

How fast can I start?

Your team is online within a minute. Add matches, share the link, and the first answers come in.
